# XoXoCom Courses

How visitors of the XoXoCom site express interest in and register for XoXoCom's hands-on A.I. courses.

## Language

**Booking**:
A visitor's request to take part in a course. It commits neither side: no place is reserved and no payment is taken until XoXoCom confirms personally. One person holds at most one booking per course; booking again replaces it.
_Avoid_: Enrollment, order, purchase, waitlist signup

**Place**:
One participant's seat on a course. A For-myself booking asks for one place; a For-my-team booking asks for several.
_Avoid_: Seat, slot, ticket

**For myself**:
The booking type of a person who books one place for their own sake, not on behalf of an employer. German label: "Privat".
_Avoid_: Individual, private person, B2C

**For my team**:
The booking type of a contact person who books several places on a course for their company's team, in one booking. The other team members do not book themselves. German label: "Für mein Unternehmen".
_Avoid_: Company booking, business, B2B, corporate

**Contact person**:
The one person who makes a For-my-team booking and speaks for the team towards XoXoCom.
_Avoid_: Admin, account owner, team lead

## Internal

Terms XoXoCom uses internally and never shows to visitors.

**Interest-based scheduling**:
XoXoCom's rule that a course only gets a start date once enough places (not bookings) have been booked for it. How many is "enough" is decided case by case, not fixed.
_Avoid_: Cohort, waitlist
