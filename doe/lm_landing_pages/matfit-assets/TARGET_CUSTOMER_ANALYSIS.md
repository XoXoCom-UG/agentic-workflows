# MAtfIT (Multi-AI-tool for IT Transformation): Marketing Campaign

**Description:**
- MAtfIT is an AI SaaS Designed to play the role of an AI coach to help product owners, product managers, developers, and IT managers in German and European IT companies achieve their goals, for example, reduce their time to market, gain greater market shares, reduce costs, uh increasing revenues, and maybe keep uh tabs on the current market strengths or area trades, keep tabs also on the competitors, and so on. So the goal is to coach them, help them devise plans, road maps as well as implementation of steps, frameworks, and sustainable/on-the-mark AI Transformation strategies necessary to promote their growth and then achieving their goals and targets.

- Developed by XoXoCom UG

**Possible Features/ Value propositions (in German):**
- Analysiere deinen Tech Stack hinsichtlich aktueller AI Implementierung

- Abgleich mit aktuellen und zukünftigen Trends

- Plane deine Umsetzung gemäß Business Value

- Auf Stories und Teilschritte herunter gebrochen

- Prüfung von Requirements zur Umsetzung (Aufwandsschätzung, Manpower, Know How, Technik, Geldmittel)

## Target customer analysis

### Target Customer Profile 1:

**Description:**
- roles = [Product managers, Product Owners, Software Developers, AI Developers, IT Managers] at IT Companies.
- Age: 30-55
- Location: Germany/Europe

**Desired Outcome**
- Expert level coaching on AI transformation and building, planning, and shipping software projects in order to:
1. Reduce time to market e.g. 4 months to 2 months
2. Increase revenue by a factor of at least 1.25.
3. Stay on top of market trends and competitors
4. Gain greater market share

- etc...

**Pain points**
- Most AI tools out there give generic AI advice, not really advice that is specific to their needs. And many times due to AI hallucinations, these tools give false information.

- Having to pay thousands of euros on HI coaches or business coaches or even qualified personnel who might not even be able to deliver on their promises.

- AI fatigue. So many tools to manage, so many tools to pay for, so many tools which have access to data can mess it up in different ways. Also having that many tools can also be a hurdle because not all personnel can be trained on using every single tool.

**Benefits**
- Research and competitor analysis that helps them stay on top of market trends and the competitors and the market as a whole within hours instead of weeks.

- One tool for multiple use cases (Multi-AI-Tool). Which means no more paying for multiple tools at once. That is the save costs reducing costs and they don't have to manage multiple tools which mess around with their data in multiple different ways.

- MAtfIT
